# Contributing

Thanks for your interest! At this stage, the most valuable contribution is **feedback**:
[open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) to report what works, what breaks, and what you miss.

## Pull requests

Navette may later become a commercial app (for example on the App Store and Play Store), which is
why it is licensed under the AGPL-3.0 and why contributions come with one condition:

> By submitting a contribution, you agree that it is licensed to the project under the AGPL-3.0,
> **and** you grant the project's author a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free right to use,
> modify and relicense your contribution under other terms, including proprietary ones.

If you are not comfortable with that, please open an issue describing the change instead — it is
just as useful.

For code changes: keep the three implementations (relay, Mac, Android) compatible with the
protocol in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md), run the tests of each part you touch, and match the
surrounding style (comments are in French).
